Beispiele — Patient:innen, Bundles, Antworten

Translated page. Original language: German.

This page collects every example of the IG in one place: three synthetic example patients, four visit bundles and 46 example responses. The use-case pages PSS, AN and NTx point here; their tables name, per questionnaire, the example response that exercises it.

The examples are not unconnected test data but coherent data sets: every response belongs to one person and one assessment visit, and the answer values are coordinated, not rolled. What is not collected at a visit is absent from its bundle rather than present but empty — that is how the TIMING column of the Item Level Dictionary becomes visible, which is deliberately not modelled in the questionnaires.

The three example patients

Patient Use case Profile Visits
pcor-mii-exa-patient-an AN female, born 1985. Anorexia nervosa, restricting type, first signs around 2018, in treatment since 2020, weight partially restored initial/screening visit 18 June 2026, monitoring visit 30 July 2026
pcor-mii-exa-patient-pss PSS male, born 1977. Persistent somatic symptoms for about two years (exhaustion, back and gastrointestinal complaints); moderate somatic symptom burden (PHQ-15 = 12, SSD-12 = 22), mild depressive (PHQ-8 = 9) and anxious (GAD-7 = 7) symptoms, moderately reduced work ability (WAI 6/10) screening visit 25 June 2026
pcor-mii-exa-patient-ntx NTx male, born 1968. Recipient of a living-donor kidney on 14 November 2024, first graft, haemodialysis in a dialysis practice before transplantation; under immunosuppression with tremor and diarrhoea as the most distressing side effects, occasionally missed doses initial assessment 6 October 2026 (screening and first quarterly monitoring at one visit)

The patients carry only gender, birth date and language; everything else is in the responses. The generic questionnaires (DEM, MHI, PROMIS, PHQ, WHODAS …) are answered by the AN and PSS patients; the NTx patient shows only the NTx-specific questionnaires.

The four visit bundles

One Bundle of type collection per assessment visit, with the patient resource and all responses of that visit. The bundles are generated from the individual responses by scripts/build-example-bundles.py and checked in; the visit assignment is maintained explicitly there — whoever adds a response enters it there and rebuilds.

Bundle Patient Visit Entries Content
AN — initial AN 2026-06-18 13 DEM, MHI, the five AN instruments and five UKHD questionnaires; UKHD-ND is deliberately absent because none of its items is collected at the initial visit
AN — monitoring AN 2026-07-30 10 nine follow-up responses six weeks later, slightly improved, not remitted: ERQ-6, EDE-Q6, ANSOCQ-2, SSUK-2, UKHD-PT (only bdkm16), UKHD-CT, UKHD-LE, the first UKHD-ND response and the metadata-only response to UKHD-EDP. ACE, UKHD-ANB, UKHD-D and bdkm15 are initial items and are not repeated
PSS — screening PSS 2026-06-25 21 the complete PSS battery: DEM, MHI, OPD-SFK, GSLTPAQ, EXPECT, IPQ-S, WAI and PHQ-D panic block as PCOR-MII questionnaires plus twelve responses to MII PRO questionnaires (PHQ-9, GAD-7, PHQ-15, SSD-12, WHODAS-12, EURONET-SOMA, WI-7, SCOFF, ISR-Z, PC-PTSD, PROMIS-16, PROMIS Cognitive Function). The full PHQ-9 is answered because the screening visit is an on-site visit with an immediate physician contact
NTx — initial assessment NTx 2026-10-06 6 MHI-NTx (screening), NTx follow-up questionnaire and the three metadata-only responses to BAASIS, ABQ and MTSOSD-R59 (only linkIds and codes, no wording)

The responses to MII PRO questionnaires are JSON files under input/examples/ and reference the upstream canonicals with the version pin |2027.0.0-ballot.2; the responses to PCOR-MII questionnaires are FHIR Shorthand instances with the pin |0.9.0. Both forms sit side by side in the bundle.

Validation status

All examples and bundles are checked with the IG Publisher and the HL7 validator against the MII PRO module: 0 errors. Not warning-free — the remaining warning classes (dom-6 missing narrative, display hints for codes without a German designation on the terminology server, language de-DE against narrative de) are known and accepted; what they mean and when they must not be ignored is on Validation, together with the three ways to check your own bundles.

Notes on the lifecycle from Questionnaire to QuestionnaireResponse are under Implementation; all artefacts under Artifacts.
