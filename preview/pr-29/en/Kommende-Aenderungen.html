Kommende Änderungen (2027 / SDC 4.0.0)

Translated page. Original language: German.

  • What the switch changed
  • What remains open until the final 2027 release
  • What is explicitly not coming

This page accompanies PCOR-MII's move to the MII PRO module 2027. With release 0.9.0 the switch is done: PCOR-MII inherits from de.medizininformatikinitiative.kerndatensatz.pros 2027.0.0-ballot.2 (SDC 4.0.0, ISiK 6.0.0) instead of 2026.7.0. The page records what that settled and what remains open until the final 2027 release — a ballot may still change until then, that is what it is for.

As of 0.9.0: dependency, every pin, the validation container and the validator commands on Validation are on 2027.0.0-ballot.2; answer weights are called itemWeight. The step to the final 2027 release is a pure version change, provided the ballot holds.

What the switch changed

Upstream change (2027) Implementation in PCOR-MII (0.9.0)
SDC 3.0.0 → 4.0.0 — new invariants sdc-base-1/sdc-base-2 (every answerOption.valueCoding needs code or display, and code requires system), sdc-behave-2 (enableWhen and enableWhenExpression are mutually exclusive), sdc-rend-1 (page control at root level only) Satisfied. The inline answerOptions (SNOMED yes/no per ADR-013) carry system and code; all other answers bind via answerValueSet. Full validation with the IG Publisher against SDC 4.0.0 without new errors.
itemWeight instead of ordinalValue — weights as an extension on answerOption (foreign terminology) or as the concept property http://hl7.org/fhir/concept-properties#itemWeight on our own CodeSystems; the module writes them only through the RuleSets WeightedOption, WeightedConcept, ItemWeightProperty Done: 15 answer CodeSystems, 72 concepts, weights and codes unchanged; the score expressions of OPD-SFK and ABQ use .weight().sum(). Same direction as our ballot submission HDB-972. Which scales carry weights at all (only ordinal ones — see UKHD supplementary items) is unchanged.
PROMIS Scale v1.2 – Global Health new in the module (mii-qst-pro-promis-global-health, 10 items, status draft) PROMISGH is declared via derivedFrom as a 2-item subset of it; linkId alignment and whether the local questionnaire survives are open (see PROMIS).
PROMIS-29 cleaned up (HDB-1007/-1008/-1009): weights, T-score tables and LOINC codes of the scores corrected, the -de and -minimal variants retired (still resolvable as empty shells) No change to PCOR-MII artefacts; the manifests pin mii-qst-pro-promis-29|2027.0.0-ballot.2. The PROMIS-29 page is updated.
QR profile: translation extension also on item.text and item.answer.valueCoding.display; the ordinalValue slice on the answer that the first ballot still carried is gone in ballot.2 Responses may carry multilingual item texts; our examples deliberately do not. The ballot comment on the ordinalValue slice is no longer needed.
EORTC QLQ-C30 variants A/B dropped (eortc-qlq-c30-variant-a/-b) None — PCOR-MII does not use the variants.
Package dependencies: hl7.fhir.uv.extensions.r4 5.2.0, de.basisprofil.r4 1.6.0, de.gematik.isik 6.0.0, hl7.terminology.r4 7.3.0, xver-r5.r4 0.1.0, crmi 2.0.0 sushi-config.yaml pins SDC 4.0.0 and ISiK 6.0.0 to match the module, the rest arrives transitively; the container preloads correspondingly more packages.

What remains open until the final 2027 release

  1. Version change 2027.0.0-ballot.2 → final: sushi-config.yaml, every |2027.0.0-ballot.2 pin (manifests, example responses, bundles), container (docker/) and validator commands — the same pass as for the switch, without structural change.
  2. LOINC supplement mii-cs-pro-loinc-supplement: check whether our LOINC references (ACE items, PHQ answer codes) should switch to it.
  3. PROMIS-GH: align the linkIds with the upstream IDs per ADR-008, or drop the local questionnaire in favour of the module definition — depends on the data collection plan (2 or 10 items).
  4. Upstream alignment of the AN long forms: with the 2027 cycle the planned PR of the AN long forms against the PRO module is to be re-evaluated (instrument and score catalogue entries, literature references).

What is explicitly not coming

  • No switch to FHIR R5. The module stays on R4; itemWeight and artifact-version are R5 backports as extensions.
  • No change to linkIds or wordings of the PCOR-MII questionnaires through the switch — existing responses remain valid because the questionnaire references are versioned. (The one exception is listed above as an open point: PROMIS-GH.)
  • The PHQ-SADS recall question (shared items, 4 vs. 2 weeks) is on the PRO module's 2027 roadmap and will be solved there, not here.

Changes to the ballot are tracked on this page; what has been implemented is in the release notes.
