AN (Anorexia Nervosa)

Translated page. Original language: German.

AN (Anorexia Nervosa) is the eating-disorder entity in PCOR-MII — alongside PSS (Persistent Somatic Syndrome) and NTx (kidney transplantation). The battery consists of generic instruments (identical across all three entities) and AN-specific instruments on eating-disorder psychopathology, emotion regulation, motivation to change, social environment and adverse childhood experiences.

The cross-entity view is on Instruments; this page describes the AN battery and says, for every questionnaire, where its FHIR resource lives. Machine-readable, the same compilation is Library/use-case-an, which pins all 25 questionnaires with versions. Rights and status of all instruments are collected on Rights and open points.

The AN battery

The Resource column links to the resource itself: PCOR-MII — the Questionnaire is defined in this IG, the link goes to the artefact page with linkIds and downloads; MII PRO — the Questionnaire arrives through the package dependency de.medizininformatikinitiative.kerndatensatz.pros 2027.0.0-ballot.2, PCOR-MII does not rebuild it; open — no artefact exists yet for this row of the data collection plan. The Docs column points to the instrument's PCOR-MII page. Priorities and frequencies of the plan are in the Domain Overview sheet of the Item Level Dictionary and in the display of the manifest entries.

All five are project-specific subsets of published instruments (for the ACE the first five questions), provisionally maintained in PCOR-MII (ADR-003), and none of them carries a score. Until release 0.3.0 the ERQ-6 counted as the exception — it was wrongly identified as the official short form ERQ-S; the correction is on the ERQ-6 page.

The subsets follow a documented selection rule

Four of the five AN instruments are subsets, and the DIZ implementation list names the rule behind them in its column "shortened version?" — identically for ERQ-6, EDE-Q6, ANSOCQ-2 and SSUK-2:

"not used in full, but only the item with the highest discriminatory power per scale"

That matters more than it looks: the subsets are not arbitrarily shortened but built on a stated psychometric criterion — the most discriminating item per scale or factor. For three of the four, PCOR-MII has checked this rule against the published structure of the original instrument: for EDE-Q6 against the four EDE-Q subscales, for ANSOCQ-2 against the two factors of the German validation, for SSUK-2 against the two opposing SSUK dimensions. It holds in each case.

One exception to know about: for the ERQ-6 the singular wording "the item … per scale" does not fit — there it is three items per subscale of the full instrument. Until release 0.3.0 this page said the questionnaire was the published short form ERQ-S and therefore the only one with a validated scoring. That was wrong — the ERQ-S consists of different ERQ items; the score has been withdrawn.

The ACE carries a different entry in the same column — "the first 5 questions" — so it is not a discrimination-based selection but the front block of the instrument (abuse and neglect without the household-dysfunction questions).

For the analysis the consequence is the same throughout: one most discriminating item per scale does not represent the scale. That is why none of these subsets carries a score — details on the respective instrument pages and in ADR-003. The answer codes carry itemWeight as a precaution so that an evaluation remains possible once a rule is agreed (open point).

Example data set

The AN example responses belong to one patient (pcor-mii-exa-patient-an) and two visits: the initial/screening visit of 18 June 2026 (bundle, 13 entries) and the monitoring visit of 30 July 2026 (bundle, 10 entries) — slightly improved, not remitted, with the first UKHD-ND response, a deliberate partial response to UKHD-PT and the response to the metadata-only questionnaire UKHD-EDP. What each response demonstrates, why UKHD-ND is absent at the initial visit and how the values were chosen is on Examples.

Not collected in AN

For delimitation, because these instruments exist in PCOR-MII and sit next to the AN ones in the overall table: PSS-only are PHQ-D panic block, PC-PTSD, SCOFF, ISR-Z, SSD-12, Whiteley-7, EXPECT, IPQ-S, GSLTPAQ and the UKE healthcare-utilisation items. NTx-only are BAASIS, ABQ, MTSOSD-R59, MHI-NTx and the NTx follow-up questionnaire.

Rights and status

According to the DIZ implementation list the five AN instruments are freely publishable; for EDE-Q6 (published German version) and the German version of the ACE (ACE-D) the rights holders' consent is nevertheless to be obtained. The UKHD groups have no documented clearance, UKHD-EDP is metadata-only. All AN-specific resources are provisionally maintained in PCOR-MII until they are officially agreed and parts can be adopted into the MII PRO module (ADR-003). The full situation per instrument, the open questions and who has to answer them: Rights and open points.
